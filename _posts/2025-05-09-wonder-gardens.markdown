This is the concept for an interactive experience about picking flowers and making bouquets for other people. The experience’s target audience is children aged 10-15 and is meant to teach the importance and impact of giftgiving through the language of flowers. Though the target audience is children, adults may also find enjoyment in the experience from its down-to-earth and relaxed nature.

A major part of the experience is the personality test. Attached is an HTML file that will allow you to take the test in a web browser. The personality test was made using Twine and features six possible personality types.
