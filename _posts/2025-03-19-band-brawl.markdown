Band Brawl is a 2D side-scrolling arcade game where the player must survive against an endless horde of enemies. Based on a true story involving the Ohio University Marching 110, the player is a marching band musician who uses musical instruments to fight angry football players.

As the lead programmer, I am in charge of the game’s core design. The thing I find most important about the game is the scoring system, as it is the game’s main competitive force. The top ten scores are saved, so players can compare their scores with others or their own past scores. The incentive to improving their skills is a spot on the leaderboard. 

This project won first place at the 2025 Ohio University Student Research and Creative Activity Expo in the undergraduate ECT block.
