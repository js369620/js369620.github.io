Trolley Problem VR is a Unity OpenXR experience that puts the audience in the “trolley problem” dilemma. Will you pull the lever?

{% include youtube.html id="VOgWeevceNQ" %}

I was our audio designer, so I worked on making a spatial audio system that would randomize audio clips to be played at different points in virtual space, as well as leading voice acting sessions. I wanted the audio to be stress-inducing, so while a train horn blares closer and closer, the victims in the experience plead with the user and scream when they are run over by the train. 

You can listen to some of the voice clips here. Be advised that some are screams, so you may want to adjust your volume accordingly.
